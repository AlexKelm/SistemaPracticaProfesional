1. tests/api.crud.test.js [3 fallan]
   - POST /api/tipo-servicio: Pool is closed (espera 200, recibe 500)
   - GET /api/tipo-servicio: Pool is closed (espera 200, recibe 500)
   - GET /api/tipo-servicio/:id: Pool is closed (espera 200/404, recibe 500)
   Causa: Connection pool se cierra antes de terminar tests
   Solución: Agregar afterAll(() => pool.end())
